This app provides accurate prayer times based on your location, an interactive Qibla compass to find the direction of Mecca, and access to the complete Quran with audio recitation. All features work offline, and your data is stored locally on your device for privacy and security.

Our mission is to provide a beautiful, accessible, and accurate Islamic prayer time application that helps Muslims around the world stay connected to their daily prayers. We strive to create a seamless experience that combines modern design with traditional values, making it easy to track prayer times, read the Quran, and find the Qibla direction.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This app stores data locally on your device, including prayer time preferences and location settings. We do not collect, share, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All information remains on your device and can be cleared at any time through the settings.

For more detailed information about our privacy practices, please review our full privacy policy.

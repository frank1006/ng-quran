Privacy Privacy policy

QuranFlow gives you accurate prayer times for your location with reminders, a Qibla compass, nearby masjids, and the complete Quran with audio recitation. QuranFlow AI answers your questions about the Quran and Islamic dates with the ayahs themselves, in English, Urdu or Arabic (sign in with Google to use it). Prayer times and the Quran text work offline, and your preferences and bookmarks stay on your device.

Our mission is to provide a beautiful, accessible, and accurate Islamic prayer time application that helps Muslims around the world stay connected to their daily prayers. We strive to create a seamless experience that combines modern design with traditional values, making it easy to track prayer times, read the Quran, and find the Qibla direction.

QuranFlow has no accounts and stores your preferences, bookmarks and cached data on your device only. To work, some features send an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to these services:

  • Prayer times: Aladhan
  • Nearby masjids: our server, then OpenStreetMap or Geoapify
  • Weather: Open-Meteo
  • Place names: OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  • Prayer reminders (only if you turn them on): our server keeps your phone's notification address, rounded location and chosen prayers, and deletes them when you turn reminders off

Nothing is sold or used for ads. "Reset Data & Permissions" clears what is stored on this device.

  • Prayer times by Aladhan
  • Quran text and audio via The Quran Project (MIT)
